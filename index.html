 How to play

Make your hand small.

On your turn, play one card, a set of matching ranks, or a same-suit straight of three or more cards. Then draw exactly one card from either pile.

At the very start of your turn, call Yaniv with 7 points or fewer. The lowest hand scores zero; everyone else scores their hand. A successful caller scores zero. If anyone ties or beats the caller with a lower hand, the caller gets 30 instead.

The first player to reach 100 ends the game. Lowest total wins.
